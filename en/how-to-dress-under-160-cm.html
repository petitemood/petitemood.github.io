How to dress when you are under 160 cm

Practical ideas for balanced proportions without restrictive rules.

The goal is not to look taller at all costs. It is to make clothes work with your frame, so waistlines, hems and volumes sit in the right places.

Start with proportions

A clearly defined waist, intentional lengths and well-placed seams often create a harmonious result. Tailoring a hem can make more difference than changing your style.

Build outfits your way

Monochrome looks, shorter jackets and high-rise trousers can be useful tools, but personal comfort comes first. Use these ideas as options, not obligations.
