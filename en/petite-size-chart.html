Petite size chart

Helpful reference points for height, trousers, sleeves and torso.

Size charts are a starting point, not a verdict. Two people of the same height may need different rises, sleeve lengths or proportions.

Measure accurately

Use a soft tape measure and keep it level. Record bust, waist, hips, inside leg and, when useful, shoulder-to-waist length.

Compare every brand

There is no universal petite chart. Always compare your measurements with the specific product and check whether the garment is designed to stretch.
