Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Petite Mood and petite sizing.

What does petite mean?

Petite usually refers to clothing proportions designed for shorter frames, often around 160 cm or under. It does not mean thin or extra-small.

Is Petite Mood a clothing shop?

No. Petite Mood is an independent information and community project.

Is Petite Club free?

Yes. Any future paid initiative will always be clearly identified before joining.

How can I suggest a topic?

Email your idea to info@petitemood.com. Suggestions are genuinely welcome.

How is my data used?

Only for the purposes described when you submit it. Read the privacy page for more information.
