What does petite sizing mean?

Petite sizing is about proportion, not weight.

Petite clothing is designed for shorter frames, usually around 160 cm (5'3") and under. The difference is not simply a smaller label: rises, knee placement, sleeve length and overall proportions are adjusted.

What petite changes

A petite garment may have a shorter torso, higher knee placement, shorter sleeves and a more proportionate rise. This helps the garment sit where the designer intended.

How to choose

Start from your usual size, then compare the brand’s petite measurements. Pay particular attention to inseam, rise, sleeve length and shoulder placement.
