How to find the best petite brands

How to recognise a true petite range and compare fits.

The best brand is the one whose proportions work consistently for your body and style. A clear, detailed size guide is more valuable than a long product list.

Signs of a useful petite range

Look for dedicated petite patterns, several sizes, published inseams and reviews from shoppers who mention their height.

Keep your own notes

Record the size, inseam and fit that worked. Brand sizing changes, so your measurements remain the most reliable reference.
