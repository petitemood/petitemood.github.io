Maxi dresses for petite women

Shapes and details that keep the silhouette balanced.

A long dress does not overwhelm a petite figure when the bodice, waist and hem are positioned correctly.

Look at the structure

Defined waists, adjustable straps and scaled prints can make fitting easier. Slits and vertical seams add movement without requiring high heels.

Choose the right length

The hem should allow you to walk comfortably. Petite-specific maxi dresses often save alterations because the bodice and waist are shortened too.
