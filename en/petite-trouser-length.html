The ideal length for petite trousers

How to assess inseam, rise and where the hem should fall.

There is no single perfect length: it depends on the cut, shoes and the effect you want. The key is choosing the length intentionally.

Useful reference points

Cropped trousers usually reveal the ankle; straight styles can skim the shoe; wide-leg trousers often work best close to the floor without dragging.

Before altering

Wash the garment as instructed and try it with the shoes you plan to wear. Pin the hem and move around before committing.
